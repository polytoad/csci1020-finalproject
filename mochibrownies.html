Mochi brownies

Last made October 10th 2026 at 22:00 by Kyu

Jump to recipe | Jump to story

Ingredients

Dry ingredients

  • 1 cup mochiko flour
  • 1/2 cup cocoa powder
  • 1 cup granulated sugar
  • 1 1/2 teaspoon baking powder

Wet ingredients

  • 5 tablespoons butter (melted)
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 tablespoon vanilla extract
  • 1 1/2 cup milk

Other

  • 1/2 cup mochiko flour
  • chocolate chips, to liking

Equipment

  • Medium bowl
  • Whisk
  • Big spoon
  • Large bowl
  • Spatula
  • Square baking pan
  • Parchment paper
  • Oven at 350°F

Instructions

Dry ingredients

  1. Sift cocoa powder into a 1/2 cup measuring cup.
  2. Add ~1 cup mochiko flour, cocoa powder, 1 cup granulated sugar, and 1 1/2 teaspoon baking powder into a medium bowl.
  3. Whisk until evenly mixed.

Wet ingredients

  1. Microwave 1 1/2 cup milk for ~50 seconds.
  2. Add 2 eggs, ~1 tablespoon vanilla extract, and milk into large bowl.
  3. Whisk until there are no egg streaks.
  4. Melt 5 tablespoons butter.
  5. Gradually add in butter into large bowl and whisk until evenly mixed.
  6. Gradually add in contents of medium bowl (dry ingredients) into large bowl (wet ingredients) and mix.
  7. Gradually add up to ~1/2 cup mochiko flour into large bowl and mix until consistency isn't too liquidy.

Baking

  1. Line a square baking pan with parchment paper.
  2. Pour the batter into pan.
  3. Preheat the oven to 350°F.
  4. Sprinkle chocolate chips to the top of the mochi brownie mixture.
  5. Bake for ~1 hour or until a toothpick stabbed into the middle comes out mostly clean.
  6. Let the brownies cool completely before cutting into.

Ah, yes. Mochi brownies. They're like, my signature baked good.

As generative AI's number one hater, I have to say... it helped me make these. Not how you think though, I still hate generative AI!!!

You see, some Christmases ago, I wanted to make cookies. Specifically, I wanted to make mochi cookies. During this time, AI was just starting up and lots of people were writing stuff with AI. Little did I know, AI has affected recipes too.

So, imagine me, searching up "matcha mochi cookies" and then clicking on the second search result. As usual, I scrolled through all the text at the beginning of the recipe (my mistake), skimmed the ingredients list, and wrote up an ingredient grocery list. There were some... weird? ingredients, but I didn't really think about them much, because mochi cookies is a completely new venture for me.

For me, I wanted to have a sort of cookie-mochi hybrid, with a gooey cookie surrounded by a mochi outside, kinda like mochi ice cream. Unfortunately...
