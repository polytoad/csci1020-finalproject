Sunbutter dog biscuits

Last made July 2nd 2026 at 15:00 by Kyu

Jump to recipe | Jump to story

Ingredients

  • 2/3 cup shredded carrots
  • 1 cup sunbutter
  • 3/4 cup milk
  • 1 egg
  • 2 1/4 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 tablespoon baking powder
  • 1/3 cup oats

Equipment

  • Bowl
  • Grater
  • Spatula
  • Rolling pin at 1/4 inch thickness
  • Cookie cutter
  • Baking sheet
  • Parchment paper
  • Oven at 325°F

Instructions

  1. Shred carrots with a grater into a bowl to make 2/3 cup shredded carrots.
  2. Mix in 1 cup sunbutter, 3/4 cup milk and 1 egg into the bowl.
  3. Mix in 2 1/4 cups all-purpose flour and 1 tablespoon baking powder into the bowl.
  4. Flour a clean surface and place dough on top.
  5. Mix in 1/3 cup oats.
  6. Flour a rolling pin.

  7. Until there is not enough dough left:

    1. Roll dough out to 1/4 inch thickness.
    2. Cut shapes out with cookie cutters.
    3. Place onto baking sheet lined with parchment paper.
  8. Preheat oven to 325°F.
  9. Bake for ~15 minutes or until bottom of biscuits are lightly browned.
  10. Take sheets out of oven and flip biscuits.
  11. Bake for ~10 minutes or until crunchy as desired.
  12. Let cool completely.
