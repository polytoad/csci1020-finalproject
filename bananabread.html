Banana bread

Last made April 29th 2026 at 21:00 by Kyu

Jump to recipe | Jump to story

Ingredients

  • 4 large ripe bananas
  • 1/3 cup butter (melted)
  • 1/2 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1/16 teaspoon salt
  • 2/3 cup granulated sugar
  • 1 large egg (beaten)
  • 2 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1 1/2 cup all-purpose flour
  • white chocolate chips

Equipment

  • Medium bowl
  • Loaf pan
  • Oven at 350°F

Instructions

Batter

  1. Mash 4 large ripe bananas.
  2. Mix in 1/3 cup melted butter.
  3. Mix in 1/2 teaspoon baking soda and 1/16 teaspoon salt.
  4. Mix in 2/3 cup granulated sugar, 1 large beaten egg, and 2 teaspoon vanilla extract.
  5. Mix in 1/3 cup white chocolate chips.
  6. Mix in 1 1/2 cup all-purpose flour until flour is not visible.

Baking

  1. Preheat the oven to 350°F.
  2. Lightly coat all sides of a loaf pan with butter.
  3. Transfer the banana bread mixture to the loaf pan.
  4. Spread white chocolate chips to the top of the banana bread mixture.
  5. Bake for 55 minutes or until a toothpick stabbed into the middle comes out clean.

And finally, this is the first baking recipe I ever followed.

I don't make it much anymore. However, me following this recipe many years ago led me to what I am today.

I first thought baking was a scary thing. I've heard horror stories of how baking is like being in a science lab, and if you mess something up even a little bit, the end result wont't taste good.

Back then, I had two very ripe bananas, some flour (and also other banana bread ingredients), and a dream.

Also, I was fueled by the fact that baked goods like to have nuts in them. I was saddened every time I went to a bakery because even if the food smelled really good, I couldn't have any of it without the risk of dying a horrible death.

Now, many years later, I can just bake my own stuff if I wanted to.
